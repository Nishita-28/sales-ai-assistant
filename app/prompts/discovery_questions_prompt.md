## Potential Right to Win

Using ONLY the approved product documentation retrieved as context, identify the potential "Right to Win" for this customer opportunity.

The Right to Win is the capability, feature, certification, technical specification, deployment advantage, or differentiator in our products that could make our solution the preferred choice for this customer's use case.

A capability only qualifies as a Potential Right to Win if BOTH of the following are true:

1. It is explicitly supported by the approved documentation.
2. It directly addresses an explicit customer requirement, pain point, operational constraint, or likely buying driver inferred from the described use case.

Do NOT list every product feature from the documentation. Exclude generic features that are not clearly relevant to this customer's situation, even if they are documented. Prioritize capabilities that would materially influence the customer's purchasing decision for this specific use case.

Instructions:

- Analyze the customer's use case together with the retrieved documentation.
- Base every Right-to-Win point ONLY on evidence found in the approved documents.
- Do NOT use outside knowledge.
