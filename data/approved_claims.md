# Approved Claims

Statements below are directly supported by the approved document corpus in `data/approved_docs/`. Update this file whenever the approved documents change -- it's read by a human reviewer, but (unlike `data/restricted_claims.yaml`) has no direct effect on what `app/claim_checker.py` enforces.

- Multi Nano Sense Technologies develops and manufactures **hydrogen sensing platforms**, designed and produced in India, already in production and deployed. Every currently catalogued product (FIXaHY, PORTaHY, AURIGA, Vision H2 LD, the H2-in-O2 Analyzer) is hydrogen-specific -- including Vision H2 LD's own MEMS-based sensor. Do not claim multi-gas detection (helium, methane, SF6, hydrocarbons, refrigerants, or any gas other than hydrogen) for any of them.
- Target application areas, per the product catalogues and historical deployment records: hydrogen leak detection, industrial safety monitoring, battery room monitoring, hydrogen mobility / FCEV, oil & gas, power plants, steel manufacturing, research and testing laboratories, and space/aerospace.
- The FIXaHY fixed leak detector series (FIXaHY-G/P/E-4220MA-RRNNVVII) is **PESO approved for Gas Group IIC Zone 1** hazardous areas, marked Ex ia IIC T6 Ga, tested at a 3rd-party BASEEFA-accredited lab per IS/IEC 60079-11:2011 and IS/IEC 60079-0:2017. This approval is specific to that variant -- the other FIXaHY catalogue variant ("FIXaHY H2 LD XX") is a different configuration with no hazardous-area marking of its own; do not generalize the approval to it or to any other product in the line.
- Company-level certifications held: **PESO** (IS/IEC 60079-11, IS/IEC 60079-0), **IP 68** (IS/IEC 60529).
- Interfaces available across the product line, depending on model: RS485 (Modbus RTU slave or continuous transmission), 4-20mA analogue current loop, CAN bus (29-bit). Wireless connectivity is not documented for any current product -- see `restricted_claims.yaml`.
- Standard product warranty is **12 months from the date of invoice** (or as stated in the order's quotation), excluding batteries and damage from accidents, abuse, abnormal operating conditions, or sensor poisoning.
- A **Comprehensive AMC** (periodic calibration, cashless product replacement beyond warranty) is documented for the FIXaHY series and AURIGA -- not for PORTaHY, Vision H2 LD, or the FIXaHY Analyzer; don't assume it applies there without confirming.
- AURIGA (portable detector) is explicitly documented for **Safe Area deployment only** -- this is itself an approved claim (it should not be deployed in a hazardous area), not a restriction on saying so.
