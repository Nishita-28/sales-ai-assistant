# Approved Claims

These are facts verified against the approved product documents. This file is for human
reference only -- editing it does not change what the assistant enforces (that's
restricted_claims.yaml). Update it whenever the approved documents change.

- Product scope: Multi Nano Sense Technologies develops and manufactures hydrogen sensing platforms, designed and produced in India, already in production and deployed.
- Product scope: Every currently catalogued product (FIXaHY, PORTaHY, AURIGA, Vision H2 LD, and the H2-in-O2 Analyzer) is hydrogen-specific, including Vision H2 LD's own MEMS-based sensor.
- Product scope: Do not claim multi-gas detection (helium, methane, SF6, hydrocarbons, refrigerants, or any gas other than hydrogen) for any current product.
- Applications: Target application areas are hydrogen leak detection, industrial safety monitoring, battery room monitoring, hydrogen mobility / FCEV, oil and gas, power plants, steel manufacturing, research and testing laboratories, and space/aerospace.
- Certifications: The FIXaHY fixed leak detector model FIXaHY-G/P/E-4220MA-RRNNVVII is PESO approved for Gas Group IIC Zone 1 hazardous areas.
- Certifications: That same FIXaHY model is marked Ex ia IIC T6 Ga and was tested at a 3rd-party BASEEFA-accredited lab per IS/IEC 60079-11:2011 and IS/IEC 60079-0:2017.
- Certifications: This PESO approval applies only to that one FIXaHY model. The other FIXaHY catalogue variant, "FIXaHY H2 LD XX," is a different configuration with no hazardous-area marking of its own -- do not extend the approval to it or to any other product.
- Certifications: Company-level certifications held are PESO (IS/IEC 60079-11, IS/IEC 60079-0) and IP 68 (IS/IEC 60529).
- Interfaces: Available interfaces, depending on model, are RS485 (Modbus RTU slave or continuous transmission), 4-20mA analogue current loop, and CAN bus (29-bit).
- Interfaces: Wireless connectivity is not documented for any current product.
- Warranty: Standard product warranty is 12 months from the date of invoice, or as stated in the order's quotation.
- Warranty: The warranty excludes batteries and damage from accidents, abuse, abnormal operating conditions, or sensor poisoning.
- AMC: A Comprehensive AMC (periodic calibration and cashless product replacement beyond warranty) is documented only for the FIXaHY series and AURIGA -- not for PORTaHY, Vision H2 LD, or the FIXaHY Analyzer.
- Deployment: AURIGA, the portable detector, is documented for Safe Area deployment only. It should not be deployed in a hazardous area.
